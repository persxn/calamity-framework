# Your learning record

Everything about you as a learner lives in this folder. It is what you sync
to your own repository and carry between laptops. The rest of the
repository is the framework and is the same for everyone.

| Path | What it is | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| `profile.md` | your answers from onboarding, and notes on how you learn | the engine (`profile set`) and your tutor |
| `schedule.md` | the current shape of your day | you and the coach |
| `PROGRESS.md` | a readable snapshot of your progress | the engine (`rebuild`) |
| `events/` | the log of everything you did: one line per answer, lecture, contest record | the engine only |
| `items/` | your practice questions, with sealed answers | the engine only |
| `syllabus/` | your syllabus for each skill | your tutor |
| `tree/` | skills you added, changed or dropped | the engine (`tree add`, `set`, `drop`) and your tutor |
| `sources/` | your courses, playlists and books, and what each lecture covers | the bridge skill |
| `notes/` | your study notes: fragments, LaTeX sections, PDFs | your tutor and you |
| `mastery/` | deep-coaching progress and study books, per topic (`mastery-coach`, `mastery-textbook`) | your coach and you |
| `work/` | your exercises and projects | you |
| `cp/` | competitive programming: plan, contest reviews, solutions | you and the coach |
| `gate/` | GATE plan, mock reviews, corrections to exam facts | you and the strategist |
| `bridges/` | small tools that connect your study sources | generated for you; read them before running |

Three rules keep the record trustworthy:

1. Do not edit `events/`, `items/` or `PROGRESS.md` by hand. If something in
   your record is wrong, tell your tutor; there is a command for it.
2. Do not open files in `items/`: they hold the answers to questions you
   have not been asked yet.
3. Never put passwords, cookies, tokens or course material in here.

This folder starts empty apart from this file. It fills in from your first
session.
