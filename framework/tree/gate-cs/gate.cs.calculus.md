## limits-continuity | Limits and continuity

## differentiability | Differentiability
level: 2
same_as: [gate.da.calculus-optimization/differentiability]

## maxima-minima | Maxima and minima
level: 2
same_as: [gate.da.calculus-optimization/maxima-minima]

## mean-value-theorem | Mean value theorem
level: 3
- Rolle's and Lagrange's theorems and the conditions they need

## integration | Integration
level: 2
- definite integrals, substitution, integration by parts, properties of even and odd functions
