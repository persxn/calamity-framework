# Topics

## grammar | Verbal: English grammar
level: 1
- tenses, articles, adjectives, prepositions, conjunctions, verb-noun agreement, other parts of speech
- pick the grammatically correct sentence or fill the blank

## vocabulary | Verbal: vocabulary
level: 1
- words, idioms and phrases in context; choose the word that fits the sentence

## reading-comprehension | Verbal: reading and comprehension
level: 2
- answer from what a short passage states or implies, not from outside knowledge

## narrative-sequencing | Verbal: narrative sequencing
level: 2
- order jumbled sentences into a coherent paragraph

## data-interpretation | Quantitative: data interpretation
level: 2
- read bar graphs, pie charts and other data graphs, 2- and 3-dimensional plots, maps and tables
- compute a ratio, change or total from the figure

## numerical-computation | Quantitative: numerical computation and estimation
level: 2
- ratios, percentages, powers, exponents and logarithms
- estimate before computing exactly

## permutations-combinations | Quantitative: permutations and combinations
level: 2
- count arrangements and selections

## series | Quantitative: series
level: 2
- find the next term or the sum of a numeric series

## mensuration-geometry | Quantitative: mensuration and geometry
level: 2
- areas, volumes, angles, similar figures

## elementary-statistics-probability | Quantitative: elementary statistics and probability
level: 2
- mean, median, mode, simple probability

## logic | Analytical: logic (deduction and induction)
level: 2
- decide which conclusions follow from given statements

## analogy | Analytical: analogy
level: 1
- complete word, number or figure analogies

## numerical-reasoning | Analytical: numerical relations and reasoning
level: 2
- puzzles on ages, arrangements, sequences and relations between numbers

## shape-transformations | Spatial: transformation of shapes
level: 2
- translation, rotation, scaling, mirroring, assembling and grouping

## paper-folding-cutting | Spatial: paper folding and cutting
level: 3
- predict the pattern after folds, cuts and unfolding

## patterns | Spatial: patterns in 2 and 3 dimensions
level: 2
- continue or complete a pattern; count faces, cubes or shapes
