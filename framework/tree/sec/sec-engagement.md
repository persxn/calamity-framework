# Scope

What the mastery standard covers. The learner's own syllabus is written from this list
and from current official documentation when they start the skill.

- pre-engagement discovery and written authorisation
- scope, objectives and measurable outcomes
- test planning and operational risk management
- evidence preservation during an engagement
- finding validation: proving a finding before reporting it
- communication and escalation during the work
- remediation recommendations that a team can act on
- retesting after a fix
- executive and technical reporting for different audiences

# Boss

Write the full paper trail for one of your lab assessments as if for a client: a scope and authorisation document, a test plan with risk controls, validated findings with preserved evidence, remediation recommendations, and both an executive summary and a technical report.

Passes when:

- the scope and authorisation document is complete and unambiguous
- each finding is validated with preserved, reproducible evidence before it appears in the report
- the remediation recommendations are specific and actionable
- the executive and technical reports are each pitched correctly for their audience, with no promised ranks or dates

# Weave

This methodology wraps every practical exercise; an unauthorised or unreported finding is not professional work.
