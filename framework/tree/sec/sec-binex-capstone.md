# Boss

Discover a memory-safety bug in a purpose-built or explicitly authorised target, reproduce it reliably, explain the root cause, assess its impact in your lab, evaluate the relevant mitigations, and produce a regression test and a research report.

Passes when:

- the bug is reproduced on demand on a clean machine, from written steps
- the root cause is established with evidence and distinguished from the symptom
- the impact is assessed honestly in the isolated lab, with its conditions and limits stated
- the deliverable includes a minimised case, a regression test, and a report that would survive technical review and respects responsible disclosure
