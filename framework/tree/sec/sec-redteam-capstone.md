# Boss

Conduct an authorised adversary-emulation exercise against a lab enterprise you own: document the attack path, collect the resulting telemetry, produce a detection-gap assessment, remediate the weaknesses, and retest.

Passes when:

- a written scope precedes the work and the exercise stays within it, with cleanup afterwards
- the attack path and the telemetry it produced are documented and reconciled
- the detection-gap assessment is honest about coverage and false positives
- the remediation is retested and the report would survive technical review
