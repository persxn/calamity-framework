# Boss

Assemble a portfolio from the five specialisation capstones and one original piece of work: an investigation you drove end to end on an authorised or purpose-built target, from hypothesis to reproduction to root cause to a report that distinguishes what you demonstrated from what you suspect.

Passes when:

- all five specialisation capstones are passed with their evidence preserved
- the original investigation is reproducible by another researcher from the written record
- the work separates demonstrated results from hypotheses, states confidence and names limits, and promises no rank, rating or outcome
- every exercise in the portfolio was conducted within an owned, authorised, or explicitly permitted scope, with responsible disclosure where it applies
