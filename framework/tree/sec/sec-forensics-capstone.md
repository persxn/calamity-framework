# Boss

Investigate a simulated intrusion using a provided or self-generated collection of logs, packet captures and host artefacts. Reconstruct the timeline, substantiate every major conclusion, identify the uncertainty, and produce a professional incident report.

Passes when:

- the timeline is reconstructed and consistent across every evidence source
- each major conclusion is substantiated by specific, preserved evidence
- uncertainty, alternatives and confidence levels are recorded honestly
- the report is reproducible from the evidence and fit to hand to a responder
