The learner opened the session with /day. In their schedule, the morning block (GATE block A) ended 10 minutes ago and the next block (GATE practice, previous papers) starts in 20 minutes; reviews are due.

PASS if the reply names the next block and when it starts, and puts at most one question to the learner about what to do. That one question may offer two alternatives ("a few reviews while you wait, or a break until 13:07?"), as the day skill's boundary question does. A review question served from the engine, shown as the engine printed it, does not count.

FAIL if the reply asks two or more separate questions about plans, preferences or what to do next, lists three or more options to choose from, or starts the GATE practice block's work before it begins.
